Choosing a hotel for a business trip is different from planning a holiday. When you visit Tbilisi for meetings, negotiations or training, a swimming pool may matter less than reliable Wi-Fi, a convenient location, a comfortable room and parking.

## Choose the location around your meetings

A central address is not automatically the most practical choice. Start by checking where your meetings will take place and how you plan to get there.

If most of your appointments are in Dighomi or northern Tbilisi, staying in the centre could add time in traffic each morning. A [hotel in Dighomi](https://hotelagava.ge/en/hotel-dighomi-tbilisi/) may suit that itinerary better. Compare the complete route before booking, rather than choosing by distance from the city centre alone.

## Check the internet before an important call

Email, document downloads and video meetings often continue after you return to your room. Wi-Fi is therefore one of the main requirements for a business stay.

Hotel Agava offers [free Wi-Fi](https://hotelagava.ge/en/services/wifi/) in rooms and shared areas, without a limit on the number of connected devices. If you have a particularly important online meeting, discuss your requirements with the hotel before arrival.

## A reception that fits your schedule

Meetings and dinners with partners can finish late. Flights and road journeys may also bring you to Tbilisi at night.

Hotel Agava has a [24-hour reception](https://hotelagava.ge/en/services/reception/). Tell the team your expected arrival time when booking. A reception that is open all night is useful, but early check-in and other special arrangements should still be agreed in advance.

## Plan where to park

If you travel in your own car or a company vehicle, check parking arrangements before choosing a hotel. Searching for a space after a full day of meetings adds an avoidable task to your evening.

Hotel Agava provides [free secure parking](https://hotelagava.ge/en/services/parking/) on the hotel grounds, which is practical for guests who drive each day.

## Choose a room for both work and rest

For a short visit, priorities often include a comfortable bed, a private bathroom, air conditioning and enough space for your routine. A longer stay may call for a larger room.

Compare Hotel Agava's [rooms and suites](https://hotelagava.ge/en/rooms/). If you expect to work in your room for several hours, ask which option best suits your needs before making a reservation.

## Arrange your airport journey

On a one- or two-day trip, organising transport in advance can make arrival simpler. You can arrange a taxi or ask about the hotel's [airport transfer service](https://hotelagava.ge/en/services/transfer/).

Transfers at Hotel Agava are available by prior arrangement, including for night flights. Confirm the details with reception before travelling.

## Focus on the services you will actually use

A business stay does not always require a conference centre. When your meetings are already arranged elsewhere, everyday essentials can be more useful:

- A comfortable room for rest
- Internet access
- Parking
- Arrival arrangements that fit your itinerary
- Easy communication with reception
- A location suited to your appointments

See the hotel's [information for business travellers](https://hotelagava.ge/en/business-hotel-tbilisi/) for the services available during your stay.

## Five questions before booking

1. How convenient is the hotel for my main meetings?
2. Is Wi-Fi available in my room?
3. Can I arrive at the time I expect?
4. Where will I park my car?
5. Which room will suit both work and rest?
