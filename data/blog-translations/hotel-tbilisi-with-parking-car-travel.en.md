Travelling to Tbilisi in your own or a rented car gives you flexibility. You can plan your route, visit different parts of the city and take trips beyond Tbilisi. It also adds an important question when choosing accommodation: where will you park?

Looking for a space in a central neighbourhood can take time each day. Consider parking and access alongside the room price and interior.

## The centre is not always the best fit

A central hotel makes sense if your main aim is to explore Old Tbilisi on foot. A trip by car may call for a different location.

Depending on your route, a neighbourhood outside the tourist centre can offer easier parking and more convenient access to roads out of the city. It may also suit travellers who prefer a quieter setting.

A [hotel in Dighomi](https://hotelagava.ge/en/hotel-dighomi-tbilisi/) is an option to consider if you arrive by car or plan trips outside Tbilisi. The best choice depends on the places you intend to visit.

## Map your regular journeys first

List the places you will visit most often. If all your appointments are around Freedom Square or Rustaveli Avenue and you intend to leave the car parked, a central location may be more convenient.

If you will travel between several districts or head towards Mtskheta and other destinations, consider your whole route. For work trips, see our [guide to choosing a business hotel in Tbilisi](https://hotelagava.ge/en/blog/business-trip-hotel-tbilisi/).

## Find out what “parking” means

A listing that mentions parking may refer to a private courtyard, a nearby public facility or spaces on the street. Ask these questions before reserving:

- Is parking on the hotel grounds or on the street?
- Is it free or charged separately?
- Is the number of spaces limited?
- Can you enter with your car late at night?
- Do you need to reserve a space in advance?

For a driving holiday, these details can be as important as the features of the room.
