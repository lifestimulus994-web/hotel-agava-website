Jacuzzi rooms in Tbilisi

Hotel Agava has two rooms with a jacuzzi, and in both the jacuzzi is inside the room itself — nothing shared with other guests, no slot to reserve. Both are designed for two people.

Jacuzzi suite

The Jacuzzi suite is for two guests — the more compact of the two jacuzzi rooms, fully equipped: air conditioning, free Wi-Fi and a private bathroom.

Super suite with jacuzzi

The Super suite with jacuzzi is for two guests and is the most distinctive room in the hotel. It is most often booked for anniversaries, the night after a wedding, or a birthday.

Who the jacuzzi room is for

Mostly couples marking a particular date — an anniversary, a birthday, a wedding night. Also for anyone who simply wants a weekend that feels like more than the usual. Both rooms are for two guests, so for a family stay the family rooms are the better fit.

Conditions and booking

There are only two jacuzzi rooms, and they go quickly at weekends and around holidays — book early. Check availability by phone on +995 597 12 12 12, on WhatsApp, or through the online form.

Payment is taken at the hotel — cash or card. No prepayment is required for an online booking.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How many rooms have a jacuzzi?

Two — the Jacuzzi suite and the Super suite with jacuzzi. Both are for two guests.

Is the jacuzzi in the room or in a shared area?

The jacuzzi is inside the room. You do not share it with other guests or leave your room to use it.

How many guests can stay in a jacuzzi room?

Both jacuzzi rooms are designed for two guests. For larger parties, see the family rooms for 3–5 people.

How far in advance should I book?

There are only two jacuzzi rooms and they fill quickly at weekends, so book as early as you can — especially around holidays.
