Jacuzzi rooms for couples

There are two, both for two guests: the Jacuzzi suite and the Super suite with jacuzzi. The jacuzzi is inside the room itself — nothing shared, nothing to book a slot for. Full description and photos on the jacuzzi rooms page.

Which room to choose

For a short visit where you mainly need somewhere to sleep, the standard room is perfectly adequate. If you want more space, take the Lux or Superlux. If the date is a special one, take a jacuzzi suite.

Agava for newlyweds

For the first night after a wedding, guests most often choose the Super suite with jacuzzi. If you have something particular in mind — decorating the room, a late check-out, some kind of surprise — mention it in the booking comment or call us and we will arrange what we can. We do not sell a fixed honeymoon package, so everything is agreed individually.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How many jacuzzi rooms are there?

Two — the Jacuzzi suite and the Super suite with jacuzzi. Both are for two guests and the jacuzzi is inside the room.

Can the room be decorated for a special occasion?

We do not offer a fixed package, but tell us in the booking comment or by phone and we will arrange what we can.

Is a late check-out possible?

Check-out is until 12:00. A later departure can be arranged in advance, depending on how full the hotel is that day.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Breakfast is not included in the room rate — you order it separately. Add a note to your booking or arrange it with reception on arrival.
