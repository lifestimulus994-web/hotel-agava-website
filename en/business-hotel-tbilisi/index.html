Hotel for business travellers in Tbilisi

On a work trip you want few things from a hotel, but you want them reliably: internet that works, somewhere to leave the car, and a person at the desk when a meeting runs late. Hotel Agava has all three.

For a work trip

Everything a working visit actually needs

Free Wi-Fi in every room

Internet is part of the room rate — no time cap and no device limit.

Free parking

11 spaces in the hotel yard, with cameras and night security.

24/7 reception

A real person overnight — a meeting that overruns is not a problem.

Laundry back in 12 hours

A clean shirt for the meeting — 10 GEL per item.

Wi-Fi for working

Wi-Fi is free and works in every room and all common areas. There is no time limit and no cap on devices — laptop, phone and tablet at once is fine.

Honestly: the speed varies with the time of day and how full the hotel is. If video calls are the main point of the trip, ask us when you book — reception will tell you which rooms are better in that respect. Details on the Wi-Fi page.

Secure parking

There are 11 spaces in the yard, free for hotel guests. The area is covered by cameras, lit at night and watched by security; the gate is not locked, so you can drive in at any hour.

Since there are fewer spaces than rooms, tell us in advance if you are driving. Full description on the parking page.

24/7 reception and flexible arrival

There is a real person on the desk overnight, not a bell by the door. On a work trip that is a practical detail: meetings overrun, flights slip, and drives take longer than planned.

Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out are possible but charged, and depend on how full the hotel is that day — call +995 597 12 12 12.

Airport transfer

The transfer is 70 GEL from the airport and 50 GEL back. The price is per car with four seats, so travelling with colleagues costs no more. The driver meets you in the arrivals hall, and a night flight is charged at the same rate.

Book one or two days ahead with your flight number. The drive takes 30 to 40 minutes — transfer details.

Laundry — for longer trips

This is the service business guests use most: 10 GEL per item, back in 12 hours. Hand it in during the morning and a clean shirt is waiting by the evening, with no shopping trip involved. Details.

Rooms for resting and for working

Travelling alone there are usually three options: the standard room for a short stay, the lux when you will also work in the room, and the superlux for a longer visit. Every room has air conditioning, a private bathroom, a work surface and daily housekeeping. Full list on rooms.

Location

The hotel is in Dighomi, at 161 Akaki Beliashvili Street. The airport is 30 to 40 minutes away and the centre 20 to 30 minutes by car.

If your business is on this side of the city the location is particularly convenient: the US Embassy is 800 metres away, and the road out of town avoids the centre's traffic entirely.

What the hotel does not have

So that nothing disappoints you after booking: there is no conference room and no business centre. If you need space for a presentation or a group meeting, that cannot be arranged here.

Also important: the hotel is settled in cash only — cards are not accepted. If you pay on a company card or need an invoice, call +995 597 12 12 12 before booking so we can discuss it.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Is there a conference room?

No. The hotel has no conference room and no business centre. The focus is a comfortable room, internet, parking and a 24/7 reception.

Is the Wi-Fi good enough for video calls?

Wi-Fi is free and works in every room with no limits. The speed varies with the time of day and how busy the hotel is — if you have video calls, ask when you book and we will suggest a suitable room.

Can I check in late at night?

Yes. Reception is staffed 24 hours with a real person overnight, so you can check in at any hour.

Can I pay by card?

No, the hotel is settled in cash only. If you pay on a company card or need documentation, call +995 597 12 12 12 before booking.

How far is the airport?

30 to 40 minutes. The transfer is 70 GEL from the airport and 50 GEL back — per car, with four seats.

How quickly is laundry done?

In 12 hours, at 10 GEL per item. Something handed in during the morning is ready by the evening.
