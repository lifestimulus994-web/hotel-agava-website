Hotel Agava amenities and comforts

There are seven services at Hotel Agava, and each one below comes with a specific price or condition — free parking and Wi-Fi, breakfast, airport transfer, laundry, a lift and 24/7 reception.

24/7 reception

Reception is staffed 24 hours, and overnight that means a real person, not a bell by the door. Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00; early check-in and late check-out are charged. Staff speak Georgian, English, Russian and Turkish. Read more →

Secure parking

There are 11 spaces in the yard, free for hotel guests. The area is covered by cameras, lit at night and watched by security; the gate is not locked, so you can drive in at any hour. SUVs, minibuses and even a tour coach fit. Read more →

Free Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi is free and works in every room and all common areas. There is no time limit and no cap on devices; reception gives you the password at check-in. Read more →

Breakfast

A buffet from 08:00 to 11:00, at 30 GEL per guest. On the table: eggs, several kinds of cheese, pastries, vegetables, olives, yoghurt, cereal, jam and honey, seasonal fruit; coffee, tea and juice are included. Breakfast is not part of the room rate. Read more →

Airport transfer

Pickup from the airport is 70 GEL and the return trip 50 GEL. The price is per car with four seats; the driver meets you in the arrivals hall and a night flight is charged the same. Book one or two days ahead; the drive is 30 to 40 minutes. Read more →

Laundry

10 GEL per item, back in 12 hours. It is priced per item rather than by weight, so you know the total in advance. Hand it in at reception at any hour. Read more →

The lift

The hotel has four floors and the lift serves all of them. It is the detail two kinds of guest tend to look for: anyone travelling with heavy luggage, and anyone for whom stairs are a problem — older guests, families with small children, or anyone with limited mobility.

If you would prefer a room near the lift or on a particular floor, say so when booking and we will accommodate it where we can.

What the hotel does not have

Plainly, so nothing surprises you on arrival: there is no full-menu restaurant, swimming pool, spa, gym or conference room. There is also no luggage room, no currency exchange and no EV charger, and the hotel is settled in cash only.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Does the hotel have a lift?

Yes. The hotel has four floors and the lift serves all of them.

Is parking free?

Yes, free for hotel guests. There are 11 spaces in the yard, so please tell us in advance if you are arriving by car.

Is the Wi-Fi charged?

No, Wi-Fi is free and works in every room and all common areas, with no time or device limit.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

No. Breakfast is ordered separately at 30 GEL per guest — a buffet served 08:00 to 11:00.

Is there a restaurant or a pool?

No. The hotel has no full-menu restaurant, swimming pool, spa or gym.

Can I leave my bags after check-out?

Unfortunately not — the hotel has no luggage room. If you are staying in the city that day, a late check-out is the better option.
