Choosing a Tbilisi Hotel with Parking

2026-11-11

Travelling to Tbilisi in your own or a rented car gives you flexibility. You can plan your route, visit different parts of the city and take trips beyond Tbilisi. It also adds an important question when choosing accommodation: where will you park?

Looking for a space in a central neighbourhood can take time each day. Consider parking and access alongside the room price and interior.

Why on-site parking matters

If you plan to drive daily, a hotel with parking on its own grounds can simplify your stay. You can return without having to look for a street space or leave the car some distance from your room.

Hotel Agava offers free secure parking in its courtyard. Before booking, check any arrangements that matter for your vehicle and itinerary.

The centre is not always the best fit

A central hotel makes sense if your main aim is to explore Old Tbilisi on foot. A trip by car may call for a different location.

Depending on your route, a neighbourhood outside the tourist centre can offer easier parking and more convenient access to roads out of the city. It may also suit travellers who prefer a quieter setting.

A hotel in Dighomi is an option to consider if you arrive by car or plan trips outside Tbilisi. The best choice depends on the places you intend to visit.

Map your regular journeys first

List the places you will visit most often. If all your appointments are around Freedom Square or Rustaveli Avenue and you intend to leave the car parked, a central location may be more convenient.

If you will travel between several districts or head towards Mtskheta and other destinations, consider your whole route. For work trips, see our guide to choosing a business hotel in Tbilisi.

Find out what “parking” means

A listing that mentions parking may refer to a private courtyard, a nearby public facility or spaces on the street. Ask these questions before reserving:

  • Is parking on the hotel grounds or on the street?
  • Is it free or charged separately?
  • Is the number of spaces limited?
  • Can you enter with your car late at night?
  • Do you need to reserve a space in advance?

For a driving holiday, these details can be as important as the features of the room.

Allow for a late arrival

Traffic, stops and other delays can make arrival times difficult to predict. A 24-hour reception is useful when travelling from another region or country.

Hotel Agava's reception operates around the clock and late arrivals are possible. Share your expected arrival time with the team when arranging your stay.

Compare the rooms as well

Parking is important, but the room is still where you will rest. A standard room may be sufficient if you spend most of the day out. A suite may suit a longer visit or a stay where you want more space.

The rooms page lets you compare Hotel Agava's rooms and suites, including options for two guests and families.

A practical booking checklist

Consider location, parking, road access, room comfort and arrival arrangements together. A hotel that works well for all five can make daily travel easier.
