Family hotel in Tbilisi

When you travel as a family, two things matter: fitting everyone into one room and having somewhere to leave the car. Hotel Agava has three family options — for 3, 4 and 5 guests — with free secure parking and a 24-hour reception, in Dighomi.

For families

Why Agava works for families

Free secure parking

Travelling with children is hard enough without hunting for a parking space. Yours is in the hotel's own courtyard, at no extra cost.

Rooms for up to five

The two-bedroom suite has two separate rooms — parents and children under one roof but with their own space.

24/7 reception

Arriving late after a long drive? Reception is open and you can check in at any hour.

Breakfast on site

No hunting for a café with tired children in the morning — breakfast can be ordered on site as an extra.

Room for 3 guests

The family room for three suits two parents and a child, or three adults. One space, with a private bathroom, air conditioning and free Wi-Fi.

Room for 4 guests

The family room for four is the classic family setup — two parents and two children. It also works well for two couples travelling together.

Room for 5 guests

The two-bedroom suite is the largest room in the hotel, sleeping up to five. Its real advantage is that it has two separate rooms: the children can go to bed early while the adults stay up in the other room.

Where the hotel is

The hotel is in Dighomi, at 161 Akaki Beliashvili Street. The district is quiet and the hotel has its own courtyard, which matters when you are staying with children. The city centre is roughly 20–30 minutes away by car.

Other services

  • Secure parking — free, with 11 spaces
  • Laundry — particularly useful on a longer stay with children
  • Transfer — to and from the airport, arranged in advance
  • Free Wi-Fi · 24/7 reception · Breakfast

Quick guide — which room to pick

  • Two parents + one child — family room for 3
  • Two parents + two children — family room for 4
  • A larger family or two couples — the two-bedroom suite, with two separate rooms
  • Grandparents along too — two rooms side by side; availability depends on the dates, so call ahead

Travelling with children — what helps

Our own yard. You park directly in the yard, so unloading the car and carrying bags in with children is calmer than it would be on a busy central street. The yard is covered by cameras and lit, with security on duty at night.

A lift. The hotel has four floors and the lift serves all of them — suitcases or a sleeping child are not a problem.

Laundry. On a longer trip with children this is the service you end up grateful for by day four — 10 GEL per item, back in 12 hours. Details on the laundry page.

Reception at night. When a child needs something at two in the morning, it matters that there is a real person downstairs.

Going into the centre and coming back

Old Tbilisi is roughly 20 to 30 minutes away by car, depending on traffic. For a family the detail that matters is that you are not hunting for a parking space when you get back in the evening — there are 11 spaces in the yard, free for hotel guests. In the centre that search becomes a daily irritation and often an extra cost. Since there are fewer spaces than rooms, tell us in advance if you are driving.

Worth asking before you book

These vary by room and by date, so we can only answer them properly for your specific dates: an extra bed, a cot, the age at which a child counts as a separate guest, and breakfast for children — which is 30 GEL, the same as for adults, with no children's rate.

Call +995 597 12 12 12 or message us on WhatsApp with the number of children and their ages, and we will suggest what fits.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How many guests does the largest room sleep?

The two-bedroom suite sleeps up to five and consists of two separate rooms. There are also family rooms for three and four guests.

Is parking free?

Yes. Parking is free and secure, in the hotel's own courtyard, with 11 spaces.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Breakfast is not included in the room rate — you order it separately. Add a note to your booking or arrange it with reception on arrival.

Can we check in at night?

Yes, reception is staffed 24 hours. Let us know in advance if you will arrive late and we will wait up.

Can an extra bed be added?

Whether an extra bed is possible depends on the room and the dates — call us on +995 597 12 12 12 and we will tell you exactly what is available for your stay.
