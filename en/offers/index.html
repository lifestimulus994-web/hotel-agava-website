Affordable hotel in Tbilisi

Hotel Agava is not in the centre of Tbilisi, and that is exactly why the price is more affordable for the same comfort. This page sets out what the room rate includes, what is charged separately, and why booking direct is worth it.

Prices, in plain terms

What the room rate includes

Parking — free

11 spaces in the yard, at no extra charge.

Wi-Fi — free

In every room, with no time or device limit.

24/7 reception

Check in at any hour, with no late-arrival fee.

Daily housekeeping

Daily cleaning and toiletries are part of the rate.

Why booking direct is worth it

When you book with us directly there is no intermediary, and that means two practical things.

You get an answer immediately. An extra bed, a child's age, two rooms next to each other, a late arrival, a space for the car — a platform cannot answer those questions. We can, straight away.

No prepayment is required. We do not need card details to hold a booking; payment happens at the hotel.

What is charged separately

For transparency, here is everything that is not included in the room rate:

  • Breakfast — 30 GEL per guest. Buffet, 08:00–11:00; coffee, tea and juice included. No children's discount — see the menu
  • Transfer — 70 GEL on arrival, 50 GEL to the airport. Per car, four seats — details
  • Laundry — 10 GEL per item, back in 12 hours — details
  • Early check-in and late check-out — charged, and dependent on how full the hotel is

How to get a price for your dates

Room rates change with the season and the date, so we are not going to print one figure here that would only go stale. You can have the real one in a single message.

Call +995 597 12 12 12 or message us on WhatsApp with your dates, the number of guests and the ages of any children. We will check what is free and tell you the price.

You can also book online from the rooms page.

Booking conditions

  • Payment at the hotel, in cash only. Cards are not accepted — bring lari
  • No online prepayment required
  • Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00
  • Arrive at any hour — reception is staffed 24 hours

Which room suits which trip

If budget is the deciding factor, the standard room is the most affordable. For a family, one family room usually works out cheaper than two separate rooms. For couples or a particular date, there are the jacuzzi suites — of which there are only two.

And one honest note: we do not promise "the guaranteed lowest price", because checking every platform every day is not something we can actually do. What we can say is that on a direct booking you are always free to ask what the best we can do for your dates is.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How much is a room?

Rates change with the season and the date. Call +995 597 12 12 12 or message us on WhatsApp with your dates and number of guests, and we will give you the exact price.

Is prepayment required?

No. No online prepayment is needed to hold a booking — payment is taken at the hotel.

Can I pay by card?

No, payment is in cash only. Please bring lari with you.

Is breakfast included in the rate?

No. Breakfast is ordered separately and costs 30 GEL per guest — a buffet served 08:00 to 11:00.

Is parking charged?

No, parking is free for hotel guests. There are 11 spaces in the yard, so please tell us in advance if you are arriving by car.

Is booking direct cheaper?

Booking direct removes the intermediary and means your questions get answered immediately. For the exact terms on your dates, give us a call.
